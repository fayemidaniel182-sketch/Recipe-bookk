Jollof Rice

Ingredients

  • 2 cups rice
  • Tomatoes
  • Pepper
  • Onions
  • Seasoning cubes

Preparation

  1. Blend tomatoes, pepper and onions.
  2. Cook the mixture for 10 minutes.
  3. Add seasoning and water.
  4. Add rice and cook until soft.
  5. Serve hot.

Fried Egg

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • Salt
  • Vegetable oil

Preparation

  1. Heat oil in a frying pan.
  2. Break eggs into the pan.
  3. Add a little salt.
  4. Cook for 2-3 minutes.
  5. Serve with bread or rice.
